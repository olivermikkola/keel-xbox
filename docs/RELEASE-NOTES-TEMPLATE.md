**Beta build of Keel Console for Xbox Series X|S**, built automatically from `main`.

**Install:** download the zip below, unzip it on a PC, then upload
`KeelConsole.msixbundle` plus everything in `Dependencies/` through the Xbox
Device Portal (Developer Mode). Step-by-step guide: `INSTALL.txt` inside the
zip, or [Install on Xbox](https://github.com/olivermikkola/keel-xbox#install-on-xbox).

The model runs on the console itself with llama.cpp. On first launch, open
**Models** (Y) and download LFM2.5 350M (~230 MB).
